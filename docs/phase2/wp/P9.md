## Notes and decisions (PLAN 1.17)

- **G7 room dim (optional, decided without the user, PLAN 1.17):** built as two glassd pieces behind the consumers' flags: top-level `roomDim` and a `dim` slab cell. Placement of the dim panel behind the window (P7) and the pass-through (P8) are not requested yet, because C7's `roomDim` flag is off by default and SM-D6 ships the window tint; the contract (§1.1, §1.4, §6) says how to use them.
- **Hole fill (R0, superseded in R1):** GL-2 showed that `hole.fill` must match the crop's surroundings, else the off-axis sliver is a crisp L-bracket. R0 tuned the test fills to the stand-in art's mean, a value the reporter cannot produce, and one flat tone still left a band (review M2); R1 replaces this with `hole.edges` and the shading below.
- **Menu morph in glassd (`morph-close`, PLAN 1.5 row "Menu morph", decided without the user, PLAN 1.17, R1):** not built in v3. Safe default: the menu's slab **materializes in place** on its `phase` while C1c's CSS clip-path draws the open morph from `--sx --sy --sw --sh` (C1c D15, built against this), and on close glassd dematerializes the slab (CSS close is instant, MO R4). Why not build it now: a glassd close morph is visible only if P8 keeps the closing slab with its source rect and P7 keeps drawing a slab whose pop has gone (`contracts/sg.md` §2: a listed slab without a sinking pop is not drawn), and no package requests it; a glassd-only morph would draw nothing on the headset. No flag (nothing to switch). To build later: a slab field `morph: {x, y, w, h, r}` (source rect, Steam px) that glassd interpolates the slab's drawn shape toward on `phase` → 0 with `sheet-out`, inside a cell sized to the union; REQs to P8 (pass it, keep the slab ≤ 514 ms) and P7 (draw it). DESIGN2 §11 still describes the glassd morph: REQ P9->P4.
- **Hole tone over art (R1, M2):** a flat `hole.fill` cannot match art; glassd now takes per-edge tones (`hole.edges`, cap `holeEdges`) and shades the hole so the shadow follows the control and fades out at the crop's edge. The data path is P6 (`fill: "auto"`) and P8 (pass-through): REQs filed. Until they land, pops over art keep the flat fill, which is the R0 look; GP's over-art pops are behind the `overArtPops` rule flag (reporter contract §3), off by default.
- **Plates over a cover (R1, m1):** they now see the cover (glass over glass) with optics 2 mm in front of it, instead of the room behind the window. No flag: it only changes how a plate over a cover is shaded, and the old look (a closed rim of bent room) failed P-42/P-45.
- REQ P5->P9 (motion tokens) is done: `glassd.cpp` includes `motion_tokens.h` (`-I../shared` in `build.sh`), the phase ramps and springs come from it, and `--selftest phase` plus `tools/test_phase.sh` check them against the header.
