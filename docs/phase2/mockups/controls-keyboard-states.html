Laser: lit, never white. Hover adds white .08 and a .12 light at the pointer, drawn under the label, at once (no dwell needed: keys never lift). Pressed (%{Modal>Touched}) is white .94 with a dark label, a glow and a 1 px “goes down” shadow. Keys never scale.

Glyph badges only in gamepad mode (html[data-lgs-vr-mode="gamepad"]): Steam's own %{Modal>ActionButtonGlyph} restyled as 22 kb px badges (30 px × .74). In laser mode they are hidden by opacity, never display: none.
