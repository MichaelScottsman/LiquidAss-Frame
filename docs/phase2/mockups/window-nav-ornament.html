Gamepad modeSteam's legends become labelled buttons: label first, the controller glyph trailing as a hint. Select and Back stay, quieter, at the end. Options acts on the focused card (lifted).
Laser modeSteam's laser-mode Sort and Filter buttons join the capsule (the current sort is shown). A / B and the duplicate X / Y legends hide: the Back circle and a direct click do the same. The More circle opens a card's menu where it is.
The frozen targetThe pointer leaves Hollow Peaks (dwelled 600 ms) and crosses Neon Orchard in 90 ms on its way down. The shell keeps the last card dwelled for 300 ms or more; entering the ornament re-focuses it, and Options names it.
