Home in native mode: plate glass, one lifted item, no ghosts
Rule (native-e2e §1, PLAN §1.7 rule 1): every Steam texel that shows content is either under opaque glassd glass or shown only at its own position. Home is windowless (PLAN §1.2), so there is no window cover. Every glass element is a plate (P9 G1): opaque glass drawn at the cover's depth that hides Steam's panel under it; the base mosaic shows Steam's content in front of it, only in declared bands.
