Home in native mode: real glass and stereo, no ghosts
Rule from the first live native run (native-e2e.md §1): every pixel of Steam's real panel that shows content is either under an opaque glassd cover, or shown only at its own position. Home has no window cover, so revision 1's popped band (+10 mm) and popped top row (+25 mm) doubled. Revision 2 gives every plate on Home its own cover shape and pops only the focused item.

Static check (AT-10b) on __LGS_SG.dump(), for every surface whose cover is partial: (1) each pop's rect, inflated by 2 px, lies inside the cover shapes (else its own slab must contain its content inflated by dz · tan 35° / 0.77 mm); (2) base-mosaic pieces lie only inside cover shapes' bounds, so nothing outside a plate is drawn twice (labels stay single).
